# NYC Atlas landmark expansion

Accepted scope, September 27, 2026: remove the visible “Search the city” placeholder while retaining the accessible search label; add sourced film, literature and music associations for major New York landmarks, particularly the Statue of Liberty and Hudson Yards. Keep the accepted map-first layout, shared compact card and one sources dialog.

## Content criteria

- Every new relationship has a traceable source and a geographically appropriate pin.
- Film frames represent the linked scene; production stills remain explicitly labeled. Screen settings, studio replicas and filming locations must not be conflated.
- Literary quotations match a downloaded original text, with source and excerpt hashes. Real edition artwork and credits remain intact.
- Music keeps real Apple metadata, artwork and user-triggered previews. Lyrics, filmed performances, music videos and live recordings remain distinguishable.
- Former venues identify the historic site, not a successor building. No private residence access is implied.
- Existing catalogs and URLs remain available. Homepage ten-item selections, UI density and the $0 Google Maps boundary remain unchanged.

## Verification

Focused catalog, search, URL, provenance and coordinate checks locally; complete quality/build/browser coverage in exact-head CI. New music artwork and preview endpoints checked for successful HTTP responses and expected media types.
