# NYC Atlas television expansion

Accepted scope, September 27, 2026: add more television series and their verified New York filming locations. Preserve the map-first interface, shared compact cards and existing search.

- Add only sourced screen/location associations with a genuine, scene-matched episode frame or explicitly labeled production still.
- Keep filming locations distinct from fictional addresses, studio interiors and places that merely inspired a show. Pins identify venues or public exterior approaches, not unsupported camera positions.
- Reuse an existing place when its physical location matches. Retain all prior content and recent image fixes.
- Verify series creators and original run dates. An explicit null end year means the series is ongoing; display “present” without inventing a finale year.
- Record source URLs, image provenance, local image hashes and the date metadata was checked. Do not imply that a private filming venue is open for visits.
- Keep homepage selections, map providers and the $0 Google Maps constraint unchanged.

Verification: affected catalog/search/date/provenance checks locally; independent review and the complete quality/build/browser suite on the final source. Verify the published page, new detail data and image delivery after release.
