# Broadway Playbill collection

Jingheng requested SIX, Chicago, The Book of Mormon and Mamma Mia! alongside the existing Two Strangers on September 8, 2026. All five use complete portrait Playbill covers with their yellow mastheads intact. `content/playbills.json` records the production page, direct artwork source and edition context. The cover editions do not indicate when Jingheng attended or which cast he saw.

Four covers come directly from Playbill’s asset service. The Book of Mormon uses the familiar leaping missionary and doorbell cover reproduced by Dance Molinari; its issue date is unverified. This choice keeps the collection’s conventional yellow mastheads consistent. The downloads were encoded as WebP without retouching or cropping. Chicago’s recorded source is the 1996 revival production, matching the Ambassador Theatre cover, rather than the older production page through which search indexed the image.

`components/playbill_collection.tsx` owns one selected-show state. In Jingheng’s later September 8 request, the five shows are explicitly his current top five among Broadway shows he has seen. They are displayed as a fan of complete cards without numbered ranks. Selecting a cover or one of the five named controls pulls that card up, straightens it and brings it forward over 550ms. Native pressed buttons retain keyboard activation and visible focus; a polite title announces the selected show. The named controls provide at least 44px targets when cards overlap. The stage reserves 570px on desktop and 450px on mobile. Reduced motion disables transitions. The initial Two Strangers selection is static-rendered.
